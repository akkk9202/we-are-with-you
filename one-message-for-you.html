Where Your Message Goes

Messages are shared with patients, families, older adults, students, and community partners through WE ARE WITH YOU projects — on each community's page, in printed materials, and during visits.

Messages are shared anonymously. A few kind sentences are enough.

How It Works

Write

Share a few sentences of encouragement through the form. No name is required.

Review

Students read each message before it is shared.

Delivered

Messages reach partner communities through their pages, QR codes, and events.
