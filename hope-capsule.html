Hope Capsule

A small collection of messages, music, stories, and reflections created for moments when someone needs hope.

A Hope Capsule gathers messages, songs, photographs, and stories into a digital collection that can be opened again and again — wherever hope is needed.

How capsules are created

Stories Gathered by Students

Created by GYCO Student Correspondents, who document and share stories from partner communities.

Listen

Stories are gathered

With permission, students interview community members and document performances and service.

Create

Stories become media

Interviews, photos, videos, and letters become digital media.

Share

Media becomes a capsule

Each collection opens through partner pages and QR codes.

Where capsules live

Every Partner Page Includes One

Each partner page includes a capsule made for that community: quiet for the NICU, cheerful for family houses, rich with memories for seniors.
