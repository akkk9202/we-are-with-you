Hope Capsule

A digital collection created for a specific community.

What a Capsule Holds

Each Hope Capsule can include student performances, letters, photographs, interviews, messages, and stories collected for one community. Partners share the collection through their community page or a QR code, so it can be opened again whenever someone needs it.

Capsules match their community — quiet for the NICU, cheerful for family houses, full of familiar songs for senior communities.

How a Capsule Is Made

Gathered

With permission, students record performances, collect letters, and interview community members.

Assembled

Recordings, photos, and letters are organized into one digital collection for that community.

Shared

The partner shares the capsule through its community page and QR codes.
