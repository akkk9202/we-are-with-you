NADO School

Learn something. Make it your own. Teach it forward.

NADO School is GYCO's approach to learning. Students learn a skill, practice it until it is their own, use it to help someone else, and keep growing through the experience.

나도

"Nado" · Korean · "Me too."

The Name Comes First

"Nado" (나도) is Korean for "me too" — the decision to participate instead of watching:

"I will learn, too."

"I will create, too."

"I will contribute, too."

"I will grow, too."

Four Steps, Repeated

Learn

Pick up a new skill — an instrument, a rhythm, a language, a way of leading.

Own

Practice until it's yours. Understanding comes from doing, not just hearing.

Offer

Use it for someone else — teach a younger student, perform for a partner community, make a video.

Progress

Teaching and serving change what you want to learn next. The steps start over.
