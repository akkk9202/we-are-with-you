We’re the students of the Greater Youth Collaborative Opus (GYCO). We rehearse, perform, teach, and bring our own ideas into the communities around us.

WE ARE WITH YOU began with a simple question: how can we stay connected when we can’t be there in person?

Meet GYCO and explore our story ↗

More about how WAWY began

GYCO was founded in 2022 and registered as a 501(c)(3) in May 2023. Its first student-centered platform, WAWY, was proposed and developed by student founders Jueon (Aaron) Kim and Yeoeun (Kate) Kim.

Students have performed at partner communities, supported refugee families with Friends of Refugees, and assembled 100 care packages for people experiencing homelessness in Atlanta.
