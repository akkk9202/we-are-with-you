Why we exist

No one should feel alone in a difficult place

A hospital room, waiting area, senior community, school, or family support center can feel isolating. WE ARE WITH YOU was created to foster connection in those spaces.

Through music, letters, song requests, teaching videos, stories, reflections, and shared activities, we create simple ways for people to feel seen, heard, and remembered.

Our goal is not only to offer encouragement. It is to build a circle in which encouragement continues from one person to another.

The platform model

One Shared Template.
Many Personal Stories.

Every partner page follows a familiar structure: WE ARE WITH YOU, a partner introduction, One Message for You, program cards, a Hope Capsule, QR access, and a community message.

This consistency matters. Whether someone opens a cancer care page, a NICU page, or a school page, they can quickly understand how to participate. The design, language, and content may vary, but the experience remains connected.

This consistency defines the WE ARE WITH YOU brand.

Every partner begins with One Message for You. From there, each community develops its own experience, which may include:

  • One Message for You
  • Song requests
  • Letters and messages
  • Music videos
  • Teaching videos
  • Patient, family, or student stories
  • Quiet music
  • Interactive activities
  • Hope Capsule
  • QR codes
  • Multilingual content

One message, many settings

Every partnership starts the same way

Whether we work with a hospital, a Ronald McDonald House, a school, or a senior community, every partnership begins the same way: someone shares a message of encouragement. That message becomes the first step toward connection. From there, we listen to each partner community and create a tailored landing page, QR system, and program experience for that setting.

Cancer center

Song requests & stories

A cancer center may benefit from song requests and patient stories that offer meaning and comfort throughout treatment.

Family house

Letters for families

A Ronald McDonald House may benefit from letters that encourage children and parents during extended stays.

Global school

Lessons that travel

A partner school in the Philippines may benefit from English lessons, music videos, read-aloud stories, and accessible learning activities.

Each partner is different, but every relationship begins with the same simple step.

The student community behind the platform

Students do more than volunteer

Behind WE ARE WITH YOU is GYCO, a student-led innovation community where students learn by developing projects that create meaningful, lasting impact. Students do more than volunteer. They identify community needs, design solutions, build partnerships, create educational resources, produce media, conduct research, and develop programs that continue beyond a single event.

GYCO is grounded in the educational philosophy of NADO School, where students learn, create, serve, reflect, and begin again through each experience. WE ARE WITH YOU is the first platform to emerge from that journey. It is not the final destination, but the beginning of many student-led projects to come.

Students continue to develop new series from their ideas. WE ARE WITH YOU is GYCO's first flagship initiative, but it will not be the last.

In our own words

Notes from the programs

On a school beyond walls

A school is more than a building. It begins wherever people choose to learn, teach, create, and grow together. NADO School is a lifelong learning community where anyone can learn, teach, create, and grow. We believe education is not limited to classrooms, formal teachers, or any particular age group. A school exists wherever people share knowledge, skills, experiences, encouragement, and hope.

Learning is not a destination. It is a continuous cycle. Each cycle begins with learning, which becomes our own through practice and reflection. What we learn gains meaning when we offer it to others, and every act of sharing creates growth that inspires us to learn again. You do not need a classroom to begin. You only need the willingness to learn, the courage to share, and the heart to help someone else grow.

On what an OPUS means

In music, an opus is a numbered work by a composer that marks a distinct piece in a creative journey. At GYCO, we extend that tradition with a broader meaning: an OPUS is a student-led body of work that combines learning, innovation, research, creativity, and service. Each OPUS begins with an idea, grows through collaboration, and becomes a meaningful contribution to individuals, communities, and the world. Just as every musical opus tells a unique story, every GYCO OPUS represents a new chapter of learning, leadership, and positive impact.

On what participation includes

Students may participate in orchestral performance, music instruction, instructional video production, community outreach, student leadership, research, media, and educational projects. Organizations may request a GYCO performance, a Voices of Love song-based experience, a Beat & Breeze teaching session, a Taps of Love teaching video, a collaborative community project, or a partner landing page. We welcome partnerships with hospitals, schools, senior communities, family support organizations, faith-based communities, educators, researchers, and local organizations. Support may include instrument donations, educational materials, printing, technology, event support, transportation, program sponsorship, and volunteer assistance.

You do not need to know everything before you begin. All you need is curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to learn with others. GYCO is not only about becoming a better musician. It is about becoming someone who can imagine, research, lead, collaborate, and turn an idea into meaningful change for others.

On music with purpose

Music was GYCO's first tool for bringing people together. It creates connection before conversation, builds trust beyond words, and opens the way to mutual understanding. At GYCO, music is more than a performance. It is a gift that students use to share kindness, encouragement, hope, and care with others. Through WE ARE WITH YOU, students transform music into messages of hope, acts of compassion, and meaningful connections with individuals, families, and communities.

On returning, again and again

GYCO values consistency. By returning to the same communities, students build trust and familiarity. Each visit is prepared thoughtfully, shared respectfully, and grounded in listening and presence. That practice became the foundation of WE ARE WITH YOU.

On words worth revisiting

Some words of encouragement deserve to be revisited. A Hope Capsule gathers messages, songs, photographs, interviews, and stories into a digital collection that can be opened again and again — in a hospital room, a family support house, a classroom, or a quiet moment at home. As the correspondent network grows, students from South Korea, the Philippines, Malawi, and future partner countries will exchange capsules and encourage one another through a global community of learning and service.

On messages that keep traveling

One Message for You invites people to share words of hope, gratitude, kindness, and compassion with someone who may be facing illness, disability, loneliness, or a difficult season of life. Together, these individual messages become part of something much larger: a growing community of encouragement. Every contribution becomes part of the WE ARE WITH YOU platform, connecting communities through hope, one message at a time.

That is how WE ARE WITH YOU continues to grow.

Students create the invitation. Partners make it meaningful. Communities keep it moving.
